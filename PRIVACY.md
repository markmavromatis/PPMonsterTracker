# Privacy Policy for PP Monster Tracker

*Last updated: September 27, 2026*

PP Monster Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

All information you enter — pee and poop logs, timestamps, notes, and location (if you allow it) — is stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. We don't operate a server, and the app has no backend.

## Location

If you grant location access, PP Monster Tracker tags each log with your device's location at the time, so you can see where your dog went. This location data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Notifications

The app schedules local reminders (e.g., a walk reminder a few hours after the last logged event) directly on your device. These are not push notifications and do not involve any server.

## Third parties

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that.
